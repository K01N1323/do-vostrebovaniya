> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: full-body character sprite for the original Russian narrative game До востребования. The existing Fedor image is solely a style and framing reference. Create a distinct new person, Semyon Serov, age 54, former bus-station porter who now helps with moving jobs. Broad face, short graying dark hair and receding temples, clean-shaven, a strong build, steady gaze and closed mouth, a guarded but dignified everyday expression. He wears a worn charcoal-blue short canvas jacket over a muted russet knitted sweater, dark straight trousers and practical leather shoes. Hands loose at his sides, shoulders square. He is currently claiming his old overcoat so he must not wear or carry an overcoat. Full figure from head to shoes centered with spare margin, viewed straight on at eye level, compact silhouette for a service window. Match the rough hand-painted gouache/oil, textured planar brush marks, quiet desaturated umber and olive accents, and muted station lighting. Genuine transparent alpha background, no floor shadow, scenery, backdrop, glow, props, text or watermark. Tall 2:3 canvas.
